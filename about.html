About Strangersdiary

Strangersdiary is an anonymous diary where strangers share the small moments of their day as personal posts. Everything here is read quietly by people you will never meet, with no comments, replies, profiles, judgement or pressure, so you can write honestly and simply be heard. Feel free to awaken your inner child and share what is on your mind, while helping us keep this space safe and kind for everyone who reads it.
